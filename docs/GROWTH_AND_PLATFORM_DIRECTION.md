# Growth and Platform Direction

## Purpose

This document defines the long-term strategic direction for platform growth,
distribution, retention, and platform expansion.

---

## Platform Identity

PropIQ is an **Investor Operating System** for UK property investors.

Underwriting is the first capability shipped — the foundation that earns
trust and justifies the subscription from day one. It is not the product's
final identity. PropIQ begins with underwriting and evolves into the system
where investors manage their entire deal and portfolio lifecycle.

The distinction matters for every product decision:
- An analysis tool asks "did I solve this calculation?"
- An operating system asks "is this investor running their business here?"

The platform succeeds when investors open PropIQ to manage their portfolio,
not only to analyse a deal.

---

## Why This Positioning Was Chosen

A calculator-first product that evolves into a platform faces a structural
retention problem. Users who subscribe to analyse a deal complete that analysis,
get their answer, and have no trained habit of returning. By the time workflow
and portfolio features are added, the subscriber cohort has already formed
their usage pattern without those features.

An operating system framed from day one acquires users who are looking for a
system — users with higher retention intent — and trains the expectation that
the platform is where they run their property business.

The underwriting engine's quality is what earns trust. The operating system
framing is what earns long-term subscriptions.

---

## Core Principle

The platform grows through:

- trust (earned by the underwriting engine)
- operational usefulness (earned by the lifecycle features)
- investor workflow integration (earned by being where the deal lives)
- accumulated value (earned by building a record of deals, performance, and decisions over time)

Not through:
- aggressive growth hacks
- opaque scoring systems
- misleading profitability claims
- feature quantity over depth

---

## The Deal as Primary Domain Concept

The primary domain concept is the **Deal**, not the Calculation.

A calculation is a snapshot in time of an analysis. A deal is a living record
of an investment opportunity — from initial analysis through purchase, hold,
refinance, and eventual exit.

Every user interaction is framed around deals:
- "Add a deal to your pipeline"
- "Analyse this deal"
- "Your deal on Acacia Road is nearing its mortgage expiry"
- "Your portfolio has 4 active deals"

Users never "run a calculation." They "analyse a deal."

This framing is enforced in all copy, UI, and product communication. It is
not a marketing decision — it is the product identity.

---

## Target User

The platform targets UK property investors who:

- are analytical and want to understand the numbers, not just see them
- own or are actively acquiring buy-to-let properties
- are managing a real portfolio (or building toward one)
- value transparency and explainability over convenience

Secondary audiences:
- HMO investors (strong acquisition signal — specific, underserved need)
- BRRR investors (deal lifecycle aligns naturally with the platform)
- Portfolio landlords with 5+ properties (highest retention potential)
- Property sourcers (high volume deal analysis, team access potential)

The platform explicitly does not target:
- Users looking for a quick free calculation
- Users who want AI-generated property predictions
- Users seeking investment advice or recommendations

## Positioning

**What we are:**
The investor operating system for UK property — where you analyse deals,
track acquisitions, monitor your portfolio, and plan refinances. Underwriting
is where the journey starts.

**What we are not:**
A black-box AI advisor. A sourcing platform. A valuations tool. A property
search engine.

**The trust anchor:**
The underwriting engine is deterministic, transparent, and explainable.
Every number can be traced to its inputs and assumptions. This trust anchor
is what differentiates the platform from both free analysis tools (low trust)
and AI-driven tools (opaque trust).

---

## Retention Philosophy

Long-term retention is earned by:

1. **Accumulated value** — the longer a subscriber uses the platform, the
   more useful their historical record becomes: deal analyses, performance
   tracking, scenario comparisons, mortgage timelines. This data is owned by
   the user and exportable; its value comes from the context and history
   the platform provides around it.

2. **Habit formation** — monthly rent updates, mortgage expiry awareness,
   portfolio check-ins. The platform becomes part of the investor's operating
   rhythm because it is genuinely useful on a recurring basis.

3. **Workflow integration** — refinance planning, lender conversations, and
   accountant reporting that reference PropIQ outputs. Cancellation creates
   friction not because data is locked away, but because workflows and habits
   have been built around the platform.

The retention ladder (from ROADMAP.md) defines the path from "analysed one
deal" to "operationally integrated." Every product decision is evaluated
against: does this increase the ongoing operational value of the subscription?

## Distribution and Growth

The platform grows through credibility, not advertising.

**Primary channels:**
- Search: investors Googling specific problems (Section 24, Ltd Co vs personal,
  SDLT on additional dwelling). High intent, clean conversion.
- Community: UK property investor communities, forums, podcasts. Word of mouth
  from users who trust the numbers.
- Content: explainer content (Section 24 walkthrough, BRRR modelling) that
  demonstrates the platform's analytical depth.

**The word-of-mouth mechanism:**
An investor who shows a PropIQ analysis to their broker, accountant, or
investing partner is distributing the product. PDF export and shareable
deal summaries are distribution features as much as product features.

**What to avoid:**
- Acquiring users who only want free calculations
- Targeting the mass market before the niche is well served
- Growing faster than the product can retain

## Explainability as Competitive Moat

No other consumer property tool in the UK:
- Shows the Section 24 tax credit calculation step by step
- Displays SDLT band by band with the surcharge shown separately
- Stores every assumption with its source and version
- Makes every historical analysis permanently retrievable and reproducible

This explainability is not a feature. It is the structural differentiation
that makes PropIQ trusted by the analytical investor and not easily replicated
by a well-funded competitor building a "better calculator."

A competitor who replicates the calculation outputs has not replicated the
trust model. The trust model is the moat.

---

## Mobile Direction

Phase 1 ships as a responsive web application. Mobile-friendly but not
native.

Future mobile considerations:
- Installable PWA for home screen access
- Push notifications for mortgage expiry alerts
- Mobile deal capture (add a deal on-site at a viewing)
- Native app only when operational workflow features make it necessary

Mobile investment is not justified until the web product has strong retention
metrics.

---

## AI Direction

AI remains:
- Advisory only — never authoritative
- Explicitly labelled — always distinct from deterministic outputs
- Explainable — the AI must be able to explain its commentary by reference
  to specific snapshot data

AI is introduced in Phase 5. It is not a launch feature.

The trust model that makes PropIQ valuable is built on deterministic
calculations. Introducing AI before that trust is established would weaken
the positioning, not strengthen it.

## Multi-Strategy Platform Expansion

PropIQ's growth opportunity is not limited to one property strategy.

The platform should eventually support the major UK property investment
workflows used by different investor types.

### Residential Investment

- Standard Buy-to-Let
- BRRR (Buy, Refurbish, Refinance, Rent)
- Property Flips
- HMO (Houses in Multiple Occupation)
- Serviced Accommodation

### Creative and Control-Based Strategies

- Rent-to-Rent
- Lease Options
- Assisted Sale
- Vendor Finance

### Commercial and Development

- Commercial Property
- Commercial Conversion
- Mixed-Use
- Planning Gain
- Land and Small Development

### Structured Arrangements

- Joint Ventures
- Deal Sourcing
- Social Housing Leases

### Investor Structures

The platform should serve investors at every stage and across every structure:

- Beginner investor (first deal, simple BTL)
- Portfolio landlord (multiple standard lets)
- Limited company investor (SPV structure, CT pathway)
- HMO operator (per-room income modelling)
- Serviced accommodation operator (dynamic occupancy modelling)
- Deal sourcer (high-volume analysis, deal packaging)
- Property trader / flipper (acquisition cost, refurb, resale margin)
- Developer (planning gain, GDV, profit on cost)
- Joint venture investor (profit split modelling)
- Commercial investor (commercial yields, tenant covenant)
- Social housing operator (guaranteed income vs open market comparison)

### Strategic Principle

PropIQ should become a multi-strategy decision platform for UK property
investors, not a simple calculator.

Each strategy will have its own underwriting inputs, calculation model,
risk flags, investor KPIs, and snapshot outputs — all built on the same
trust-first, deterministic, immutable infrastructure established in the
initial underwriting engine.

This is strategic direction only. No strategy-specific implementation
detail is included here.

## Long-Term Platform Value

The platform builds long-term value through:

- **Historical depth** — investors' deal history, performance data, and
  portfolio records grow more meaningful over time. Users own this data
  and can export it; the value comes from having it all in one place with
  full context and calculation history.
- **Trust compound** — each correct analysis deepens the investor's trust
  in the platform's numbers. Trust earned early compounds across every
  subsequent deal.
- **Workflow integration** — investors who run their portfolio through PropIQ
  develop operational workflows around it. Changing tools has a real cost
  in re-establishing those workflows, not from any restriction on data access.
- **Professional network** — team access, advisor sharing, and professional
  tooling create organisational value that extends beyond any individual user.
