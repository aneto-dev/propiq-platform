# Investor Profile Direction

# Purpose

This document defines the future investor profile and strategy modelling direction for the PropIQ platform.

Different investors evaluate deals differently.

A “good deal” is not universal.

The platform should eventually support investor-specific underwriting preferences and strategy profiles.

---

# Core Principle

The deterministic underwriting engine remains objective.

Investor profiles influence:

* warnings
* thresholds
* scenario interpretation
* workflow preferences
* portfolio analysis

Investor profiles must not alter deterministic calculation formulas.

---

# Potential Investor Strategy Types

Future strategy types may include:

* cash flow investor
* appreciation investor
* BRRR investor
* HMO investor
* flip investor
* low-risk investor
* retirement investor
* portfolio scaling investor

---

# Future Investor Preferences

Potential future preferences may include:

* minimum cash flow
* target yield
* maximum LTV
* preferred regions
* preferred property types
* preferred ownership structure
* refinance strategy
* minimum stress-test coverage
* minimum DSCR
* risk tolerance

---

# Future Intelligence Usage

Investor profiles may eventually influence:

* risk flag severity
* workflow suggestions
* scenario prioritisation
* refinance recommendations
* portfolio diversification warnings
* opportunity ranking
* strategy-specific insights

Examples:

* “This deal may not fit your minimum cash flow target.”
* “This property exceeds your preferred leverage range.”
* “This BRRR scenario aligns with your refinance strategy.”

---

# Architectural Boundary

Investor profiles must not compromise:

* deterministic calculations
* historical reproducibility
* immutable snapshots
* explainability

Profiles influence interpretation and workflow behaviour rather than authoritative calculations.

---

# Future Direction

Future versions may eventually support:

* investor onboarding
* strategy templates
* portfolio goal tracking
* risk appetite modelling
* strategy comparison
* collaborative investment teams
* broker/advisor profiles

The platform direction remains:

* investor-aware
* workflow-aware
* trust-first
* deterministic at the core
