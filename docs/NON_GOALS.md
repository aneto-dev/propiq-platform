# Non-Goals

# Purpose

This document defines what the PropIQ platform intentionally does not attempt to become.

Defining non-goals is important for:

* architectural clarity
* scope control
* operational trust
* implementation discipline
* product consistency

---

# Core Principle

The platform prioritises:

* deterministic underwriting
* explainable intelligence
* investor workflow support
* operational trust

The platform intentionally avoids becoming an opaque black-box recommendation system.

---

# Non-Goals

## The Platform Is Not Financial Advice

The platform provides:

* calculations
* analysis
* workflow support
* scenario modelling
* informational intelligence

The platform does not provide regulated financial advice.

Users remain responsible for financial decisions.

---

## The Platform Does Not Replace Accountants

The platform may eventually provide:

* tax modelling
* tax optimisation insights
* ownership comparisons
* explainability tooling

However:

* accountant review remains important
* tax rules are complex and jurisdiction-specific
* platform outputs are informational

---

## The Platform Does Not Replace Solicitors

The platform may eventually surface:

* planning concerns
* Article 4 warnings
* title concerns
* regulatory intelligence

However:

* legal review remains external
* platform intelligence may be incomplete
* regulatory datasets may change

---

## The Platform Does Not Guarantee Investment Outcomes

The platform analyses assumptions and scenarios.

Real-world outcomes remain uncertain.

Examples:

* interest rate changes
* market movements
* tenant issues
* planning changes
* lender criteria changes
* operational costs

The platform improves decision-making but cannot eliminate investment risk.

---

## AI Is Not Authoritative

AI-generated outputs are advisory only.

AI may:

* explain
* summarise
* compare
* suggest scenarios

AI must never become the authoritative calculation engine.

---

## The Platform Avoids Hidden Scoring Systems

The platform intentionally avoids opaque “deal scores” without explainable reasoning.

Users should understand:

* why a warning exists
* why a result changed
* which assumptions drive outcomes

---

## The Platform Avoids Silent Assumption Mutation

Historical assumptions and configuration versions must never silently change.

Recalculation always creates new immutable snapshots.

---

## The Platform Does Not Optimise Through Hidden Logic

Future optimisation intelligence must remain:

* explainable
* user-visible
* auditable
* optional

The platform must never silently manipulate outputs to maximise perceived profitability.

---

# Long-Term Direction

The platform aims to become:

* a trust-first investor operating system
* a deterministic underwriting platform
* an explainable intelligence platform
* an operational workflow platform

The platform does not aim to become:

* a black-box prediction engine
* a guaranteed investment advisor
* an opaque AI recommendation system
* an unexplainable scoring platform
